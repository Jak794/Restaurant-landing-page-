Our Story

Twelve Years Around One Fire

Ember & Oak began as a single wood-burning hearth and a simple idea: let the fire do the talking. Every dish that leaves our kitchen touches an open flame — grilled, charred, smoked, or roasted over oak and applewood sourced from local orchards.

Chef Elena Marchetti leads a team devoted to seasonal menus, hand-selected produce, and recipes passed down through three generations — reimagined for the modern table.
